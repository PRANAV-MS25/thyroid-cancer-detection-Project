About Our System

The AI Thyroid Detector is an advanced diagnostic assistance system designed to analyze ultrasound imagery of thyroid nodules. Utilizing state-of-the-art deep learning architectures (including custom CNNs and EfficientNet models), the platform provides rapid initial screening evaluations to aid clinical specialists and patients.

Our objective is to streamline risk stratification, making preliminary evaluations faster, safer, and consistently reliable.
