Thyroid Nodule Classification

Detailed medical overview of all nodule types identified by our AI engine.

Benign Nodule

Risk: Very Low

Benign nodules are harmless, non-cancerous growths. Most patients require only routine follow-up scans with no major medical intervention.

Stage 1 Nodule

Risk: Low

Early-stage tissue irregularity. These nodules typically need periodic evaluation, but major health concerns are uncommon at this stage.

Stage 2 Nodule

Risk: Moderate

Indicates increased growth activity or structural changes. Consultation with a thyroid specialist is advised for early intervention.

Stage 3 Nodule

Risk: High

High-risk nodules showing significant signs of malignancy. Immediate specialist evaluation and diagnostic testing are strongly recommended.
